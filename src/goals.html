Description

The Lambton Model United Nations (MUN) is a student-led organization that simulates the workings of the United Nations. Our group provides a platform for students to engage in diplomacy, debate, and negotiation on global issues. Through realistic simulations of UN committees, members develop a deep understanding of international relations, the complexities of global governance, and the importance of collaborative problem-solving.


Vision

Our vision is cultivating a community of informed, empathetic, and proactive global citizens. We aim to inspire students to become leaders committed to addressing the world's most pressing challenges through diplomacy, dialogue, and mutual respect.

Mission

Educate and Empower: Provide students with the knowledge, skills, and confidence to engage effectively in international diplomacy and decision-making processes.


Foster Understanding: Promote a deeper understanding of global issues, cultural diversity, and the principles of the United Nations.


Develop Skills: Enhance students' skills in public speaking, critical thinking, negotiation, and teamwork through active participation in MUN conferences and activities.


Build Community: Create a supportive and inclusive environment where students can share their ideas, experiences, and perspectives, fostering a sense of global citizenship and community.



By participating in the Lambton MUN, students will gain valuable insights into the workings of international institutions, build lasting friendships, and develop a lifelong commitment to global peace and justice.
